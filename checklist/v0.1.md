## AOUT self-check

- **Atomic:** Each row asks one question and avoids combining checks with “and.”
- **Observable:** Each answer can be determined by inspecting the SRS; do not assume unstated behavior.
- **Unambiguous:** The item states the specific information or quality being checked.
- **Traceable:** The final column records the requirement, stakeholder, or clinic risk motivating each item.

**Answer key convention:** Yes = requirement is explicit and adequate for the question; No = it is missing, vague, or conflicting; N/A = the check genuinely does not apply. If uncertain, add `?` after the answer and explain the uncertainty in the evidence.
